# Data Breach

**Owner:** privacy incident lead. **Trigger:** credible unauthorized access,
disclosure, alteration, loss, or cross-tenant/cross-border exposure.

1. Start the restricted breach record; engage security, privacy, legal, and the
   incident commander. Preserve evidence and legal holds.
2. Contain affected accounts, connectors, exports, and model capabilities
   without deleting data.
3. Determine affected tenant, jurisdiction, data categories, subjects, systems,
   recipients, earliest/latest time, and integrity impact.
4. Preserve access/audit logs and checksum evidence. Redact direct identifiers
   from general incident channels.
5. Legal/privacy owners decide notification duties and deadlines; operators do
   not make jurisdictional assumptions.
6. Recover credentials and systems, validate tenant isolation and audit
   continuity, then document residual risk and notification decisions.

No breach record is closed until legal/privacy owners record their decision.
